# Competitors for the remote-hiring buyer (UK/EU first, US second)

Desk research for SydCrest. All pages seen on **14 Nov 2026** using public web search and page fetches only. No sign-ups, forms or contact.

**Evidence grades used below**
- **[O]** Official page, fetched and read on 14 Nov 2026.
- **[O-s]** Official page, seen only as a search-result snippet (the page blocked fetching, e.g. HTTP 403).
- **[3P]** Third-party source (comparison blog, press, review site). Many comparison blogs are written by competing vendors, so treat their prices as indicative.
- **unverified**: claim found only in a third-party source, or sources conflict, or nothing was found.

## 2. Notes per competitor

**Toptal.** Elite positioning ("top 3%"), all levels shown are senior. The official FAQ confirms only the $79/month subscription and a trial of up to 2 weeks. The widely quoted $500 deposit was not on the FAQ I read, so it is **unverified**. Hourly rates are not published. Not a junior competitor, but it sets buyers' expectation of a no-risk trial.

**Andela.** The homepage no longer leads with "hire developers". It is now an AI adoption, training and delivery partner for CIOs and CTOs ("You bought the AI. We make it pay off."). In 2019 it laid off 420 junior developers in Nigeria, Uganda and Kenya, saying global demand skewed senior (TechCabal, Nairametrics, Pulse Ghana, 2019). No 2024–26 layoff source found. Monthly price estimates of $6k–$15k come only from competitor blogs: **unverified**.

**Turing.** Pivoted. The homepage targets experts for AI-training work ("Your expertise builds AI"), and /hire-developers now shows "Accelerate superintelligence". No client pricing. Effectively out of the junior-talent market.

**Arc.dev.** The most transparent vetted network. Freelance $15–$110+/hr; full-time 20% of salary; 2-week risk-free freelance trial. Its 20% placement fee matches UK agency norms.

**Lemon.io.** $60–$90/hr typical, prepaid subscription billing, 160-hour minimum, 1.2% acceptance, interview by a senior engineer. Explicitly senior. A clear price ceiling reference for "vetted remote dev".

**Remotely Works.** LatAm to US startups, mid/senior, flat per-developer fee with a transparent model. Its own blog claims the transparent model cut take rates from 40–70% to around 20%. The site was live, with a 2025 copyright. Not African, but a useful model for transparent pricing.

**Second Talent.** Asia-based, so not African, but it publishes **$23–$39/hr all-in** (page dated 13 Oct 2026). This is the price a US startup will compare SydCrest against.

**Gebeya.** The homepage is now an AI platform (Dala Studio, Jitume AI). It says it *began* in 2016 as "Africa's first curated EdTech and talent marketplace". No hiring service is shown. Treat it as having exited the market. A 2026 Techeconomy item describes a freelancer search feature, so the marketplace may still exist off the homepage: **unverified**.

**Decagon.** Nigerian trained engineers, sold as a managed remote team to companies in London, Berlin, Rome, NYC and Austin. Claims top 0.5% selection. No prices. Older sources mention 2-year contracts. It is the closest African "train then export" model, but for engineers only, with no data or marketing.

**Which African/offshore players actually sell to international buyers?** Found selling internationally: Decagon (engineers; London, Berlin, Rome, US), AmaliTech (as its own staff; Germany-led, EU/US), Breedj (Europe, N. America), Remote Choice and Remote Recruitment (UK buyers, South African talent). PLP Talent Hub: employer regions unclear. Local only, as far as found: Blossom, Moringa, ALX, AltSchool, Tech4Dev, Semicolon, Developers in Vogue, Shortlist, MEST. Gebeya has exited.

**Semicolon / AltSchool / Moringa / ALX / Tech4Dev.** All are training-first. Their employer offers are either free or undisclosed, and mostly aimed at local employers. Moringa earns a placement fee (amount not published). ALX reports 63% employed in 6 months but shows no international placement product. AltSchool's own job posting mentions "Hire-A-Dev / Hire A Grad" for entry-level engineers, but a 2026 article says AltSchool "does not own job matching". None publishes an international hire price. **None found selling Ghanaian junior data or marketing talent to UK/EU employers.**

**Power Learn Project Talent Hub.** The only African player found that explicitly offers **junior to mid** talent, including data analysts, matched in days. No prices and no stated employer regions. Watch it.

**Breedj.** EOR from **€390/month per talent** (the FAQ is in French; a search snippet wrongly said $390). Roles overlap SydCrest's: data analysts and sales/marketing assistants. Talent comes from Francophone and East Africa, not Ghana. This is the price floor a European buyer may find.

**Remote Choice / Remote Recruitment (UK).** The same shape as SydCrest's bet: UK buyer, offshore staff (South Africa), marketing among the roles, "pay only when you hire", "no permanent recruitment fees", "save up to 50%". Monthly prices are not published. These are the closest structural comparables for a UK SME buyer. A search snippet says Remote Recruitment contracts talent through itself as employer of record (attribution to a specific posting is unverified).

**AmaliTech.** Proof that the Ghana-to-Germany channel works. It is a German not-for-profit (gGmbH) that runs free 3–9 month training, then employs graduates in Takoradi, Accra, Kumasi and Kigali to deliver projects for EU/US clients. This is outsourcing, not placement. It shows EU buyers already trust Ghanaian delivery, but no prices are public.

**Blossom Academy.** The most direct Ghana overlap: **Data Analytics and Digital Marketing**, both with internships. Its hire page places graduates **"at no cost"** to employers, who must pay a "fair stipend". It claims 85% placement within 3 months (self-reported). Employers shown are mostly local or regional (Ecobank, MTN, Fidelity, mPharma, Microsoft).

**MEST, Developers in Vogue.** Not talent-export competitors. MEST trains and funds founders. Developers in Vogue is a small NGO placing women developers locally (Chipper Cash partnership).

**Shortlist.** Kenyan and Indian executive search plus "Futures" for mass hiring, aimed at local markets. No SHL acquisition found. It acquired Spire Education in 2017.

**Not found:** Kinnect (no matching company), "Talent Africa" (no matching product), and Workforce Africa (only a guest article describing it as an Africa market-entry and offshoring provider). All **unverified**.

## 3. Implications for SydCrest

1. **The junior slot is empty at the brand level.** The networks buyers know have moved up-market or out: Andela (AI delivery; cut juniors in 2019), Turing (AI training data), Toptal (top 3%), Lemon (1.2% acceptance, senior interview), Gebeya (AI platform). None sells *junior* talent to UK/EU buyers.

2. **On SydCrest's exact tracks, nobody exports from Ghana.** Blossom has the same tracks but sells locally, for free plus a stipend. AmaliTech exports from Ghana, but as its own delivery teams in engineering and data, not marketing, and not placement. Decagon exports engineers only, from Nigeria. Breedj and the UK–South Africa recruiters sell marketing and data roles to European and UK buyers, but not from Ghana and not with a training or mentoring story.

3. **SydCrest's edge has to be the training and mentoring proof.** Rivals in the low-price band (Breedj, Remote Choice) sell low cost and compliance. Elite networks sell selectivity. "Junior, but trained on your stack, mentored and with checked work, plus a no-risk trial" is not claimed by anyone found.

4. **Price anchors a buyer will reach for:**
   - Floor: **Breedj from €390/month** per talent (EOR, admin, data and marketing assistants).
   - Low cost from Asia: **Second Talent $23–$39/hr all-in** (~$4k–$6.8k/month full-time; my conversion at ~173 hrs/month, not their figure. Their own published senior monthly price of $3,599–$5,999 sits below that conversion, so the page is internally inconsistent).
   - Open market: **Arc from $15/hr**; Upwork adds 5–10%; Contra adds up to 5% (cap $29).
   - Vetted senior ceiling: **Lemon $60–$90/hr**; Toptal and Andela higher (unverified).
   - A SydCrest monthly rate for a full-time junior probably has to sit **well under $23/hr (~$4k/month)** and well above €390/month to signal quality. The middle (~$1k–$2.5k/month) is unclaimed among the sources found. That band is an inference, not a published price.

5. **If SydCrest charges a placement fee instead:** UK contingency is 15–20% (up to 25%) with a guarantee tied to a ~3-month probation, usually as a sliding rebate. Arc charges 20%. On a £25k–£35k UK-equivalent junior salary, 15–20% is **£3.75k–£7k**. But a remote Ghanaian salary will be much lower, so a percentage fee yields little. A flat fee or monthly margin probably fits better. Expect buyers to want a **free trial of up to 2 weeks** (Toptal and Arc both offer one) and a **replacement guarantee of about 12 weeks**.

6. **Beware the local anchor.** Ghanaian employers are used to free placement (Blossom). The paying buyer is international, so pricing and messaging should target UK/EU SMEs, not local firms.

7. **Models worth studying:** Remote Choice and Remote Recruitment (UK SME buyer, offshore contractor or EOR, "pay only when you hire") for go-to-market. Remotely Works for transparent flat-fee pricing as a trust signal. PLP Talent Hub as the nearest junior-to-mid African analogue.
