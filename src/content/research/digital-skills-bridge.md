# Research: Digital Skills Bridge (`digital-skills-bridge`)

Issue #84. Researched and written 25 October 2026. Everything in the outline and lesson
content is original. roadmap.sh was not opened, searched or used, and no "<field> roadmap"
style searches were run. No logins; LinkedIn was not scraped. Job adverts were read for
which skills appear; no advert text is reproduced here or in the content. Salary figures
seen on some listings were ignored and appear nowhere in the content.

## Who the track is for

People with very low digital confidence, usually on a phone, who want either an entry
level job that needs everyday digital skills (office assistant, front desk, customer
service, shop attendant, field sales, NGO field staff or enumerator) or a seat on
SydCrest's Delta Mentoring Program (DMP). The track must end exactly where DMP week 1
begins (`src/content/dmp/curriculum.ts`): computer hardware, files and folders, safe
browsing and phishing, and Gmail with attachments, with a Google Docs assignment. Week 2
covers Drive, Docs, Slides and Sheets; week 3 covers Facebook pages, passwords, privacy
and judging sources. The bridge therefore teaches the layer underneath those weeks and
does not re teach them.

## Framework skills

**DigComp 3.0 (F1)**, five areas and the competences this track draws on at a foundation
level:

* Information search, evaluation and management: browsing, searching and filtering;
  evaluating information; managing information (finding and organising files).
* Communication and collaboration: interacting through digital technologies; sharing;
  digital behaviour (etiquette); managing digital identity.
* Content creation: developing simple digital content (a clear message, a filled form).
* Safety, wellbeing and responsible use: protecting devices; protecting personal data and
  privacy; supporting wellbeing (screen and study balance).
* Problem identification and solving: solving simple technical problems (storage, data,
  WiFi); identifying and addressing your own digital competence needs (self check).

**ESCO (F2)** labels digital skills using DigComp 2.2, so it adds no separate list at this
level; it confirms that the DigComp areas are the right frame for a citizen level track.

**O*NET Office Clerks, General (F3)**: work activities include working with computers to
enter data, communicating with people inside and outside the organisation by phone, in
writing and by email, documenting and recording information, and processing paperwork.
Basic skills listed are active listening, reading, speaking, writing and critical thinking.
Technology examples include office suites, email, word processing, spreadsheets and cloud
file sharing.

**GCFGlobal LearnFree topic list (F4)**: series exist for Computer Basics, Mouse Tutorial,
Typing Tutorial, Internet Basics, Internet Safety, Search Better, Using the Web to Get
Stuff Done, Gmail, Google Drive, Communication Skills, Business Communication, Money
Basics, Job Applications and Workplace Basics. There is no dedicated smartphone basics
series, which is one reason this track starts on the phone. Only series names were read;
no text or ordering was used.

## What the adverts demanded that the old outline lacked, and what changed

The draft outline (issue #43) was strong on phone set up, safety and study habits but
thin on the everyday communication and record skills the adverts ask for most.

| Gap found | Change made |
|---|---|
| Phone calls were in 8 of 20 adverts; the outline had nothing on them | Added `phone-calls-at-work` |
| WhatsApp for work was in 6 adverts; not in the outline | Added `whatsapp-for-work` |
| The readiness check asked learners to join a video call but no node taught it; DMP and mentoring use calls | Added `video-calls` |
| Written English and clear writing in 9 adverts; the English node sat under "learning online" | Moved and renamed `english-for-learning` to `clear-english` under a new communication section, with writing for work added |
| Records in 14 adverts | Kept `money-records`, reframed it around sales and spending records and moved it into a new `learning-and-earning` section |
| Confidential information in 3 adverts; DigComp personal data and digital identity; many learners share phones | Added `privacy-and-shared-phones` |
| Attaching documents (every application) and DigComp managing information; DMP week 1 starts files and folders on a computer | Added `files-on-your-phone`, phone level only |
| Forms and mobile data collection in NGO field adverts (S18, S19), plus job and course application forms | Added `online-forms` |
| Very low confidence learners need gestures and WiFi or data toggles before anything else | Added `phone-first-steps` |
| Section "Learning online" had only two remaining children after the moves | Merged into `files-and-forms` (searching) and `learning-and-earning` (study habits); section `digital-work` renamed `learning-and-earning` |

Advert demands deliberately **not** added: CRM and ticketing systems (4 adverts) and
Word, Excel and Sheets (13) are taught in DMP weeks 1 and 2 or later tracks, so the bridge
only gets learners to the keyboard and the email account those weeks assume. Social media
responses (3) are covered in DMP weeks 3 and 5. Node count went from 22 to 30.
