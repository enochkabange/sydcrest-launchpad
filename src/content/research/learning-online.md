# Research: Learning online (`learning-online`)

Issue #294. Researched and written 7 November 2026. Everything in the outline and lesson
content is original. roadmap.sh was not opened, searched or used, and no "<field> roadmap"
style searches were run. No logins; no site that forbids scraping was scraped. Platform
pages were read for what each platform offers and how its free options and certificates
work; no course text, structure or ordering was copied. Platforms are named in the lessons
as examples only, never as endorsements, and every mention of a free option, a price or a
certificate rule tells the learner that it can change and points to the official page.

## Who the track is for

Adults who can already use a phone or computer for everyday things (the level the Digital
Skills Bridge ends at) but have never studied online on their own: people working, caring
for family or between jobs in Tamale, Bolgatanga, Wa, Navrongo, Nalerigu, Walewale and
similar towns, usually on prepaid data and often on a shared device. They need to choose a
course, finish it, prove what they learned and avoid being cheated, before they commit to a
career track. The track ends with a written four week plan whose first session is done,
and a choice of the SydCrest track to take next.

## Job adverts

This is a foundation track with no job of its own, so no adverts were analysed for it and
every section has an empty `jobs` list. The case for learning digital skills at all comes
from the Bridge's 20 adverts (F2). Certificates are discussed as evidence for those jobs,
never as a guarantee of one.

## What the platforms showed

* **Free to study, sometimes paid to certify.** Microsoft Learn modules (P3), freeCodeCamp
  (P5), GCFGlobal (P6) and Khan Academy (P7) are free to study. Coursera and edX (P8 to
  P10) usually let you study many courses free through an audit option, with graded work
  and the certificate kept for paying learners. Microsoft Certifications are earned by exam
  (P4); the lessons do not state exam prices and send learners to the official page.
* **How a credential is earned matters.** Microsoft separates exam based Certifications
  from lab based Applied Skills (P4); Skillshop has product certifications such as Google
  Ads (P2); freeCodeCamp certifications need projects (P5). The certificates lesson turns
  this into one test: was it earned by passing something that checked the skill?
* **Offline and low data options exist but vary.** YouTube allows downloads in Ghana in its
  app (P12), with download quality and WiFi only settings, while in most places downloads
  need Premium (P13); quality can be lowered per video or by default for mobile data in the
  app (P14). Lessons say "if your app offers it" rather than promising a feature.
* **DataCamp is given through organisations (P11).** The lesson says SydCrest programme
  learners get access from February 2027 (I2) and that individuals cannot simply sign up for
  it.
