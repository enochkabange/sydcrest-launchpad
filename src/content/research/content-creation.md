# Research: Video and Content Creation track

Track file: `src/content/roadmaps/content-creation.ts` (roadmap slug `content-creator`, track `content-creation`).
Lesson content: `src/content/roadmaps/content/content-creation.content.ts`.
Researched 25 October 2026 for Wave 3 (new track; there was no earlier outline).

## Method and provenance

* Job adverts were read for which skills and tools appear. No advert text is copied anywhere in the roadmap or lessons; the tables below record keywords only.
* Many Jobberman listing URLs redirect to a category page. Where that happened we used the category listing cards (title, employer, short skill tags) or the search result snippet for the specific advert. The depth column says which.
* No roadmap.sh pages, no "field roadmap" searches, no logins, no LinkedIn pages. A remote content producer post seen only on LinkedIn was excluded.
* Salary figures shown on some adverts and search snippets were deliberately not recorded and are not used in lessons.
* The ESCO portal and API redirected to an EC "sorry" page on 2026-10-25 (the same outage other Wave tracks recorded), so the UK Skills England Content Creator occupational standard and O*NET were used as the skills frameworks instead.
* Platform facts in lessons (YouTube Partner Program eligibility and country list, captions, branded content disclosure, Audio Library) were checked against official YouTube Help pages on the day. TikTok creator payout country lists were only available from third party blogs that disagreed with each other; the lesson therefore says only that such programmes run in certain countries, that Ghana did not appear on the lists we checked, and that learners must check official pages.

* Review (25 October 2026, independent reviewer): the YouTube Partner Program overview page (answer 72851) was re read. It lists 1,000 subscribers with either 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, plus living in an available country, no active Community Guidelines strikes, 2 Step Verification, advanced features access and a linked AdSense for YouTube account. The availability page (answer 7101720) lists Ghana. The lesson describes these without numbers because thresholds change, and sends learners to the official page.
* Review: TikTok's official Creator Rewards support page (tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581821550694013452) renders only with JavaScript and could not be read by automated tools; a web search found only third party lists, which still disagree (seven, eight or twelve countries; none listed Ghana). The lesson now says only that such programmes run in certain countries, that Ghana may not be one, and that blog lists should not be trusted.
* Review: advert evidence is thin for several topics. Lighting, composition, storyboards, sound, analytics reporting and file organisation each appear in at most one advert we could read, partly because listing cards show only a few tags. The reviewer softened lesson openings that said adverts asked for these "again and again" or "often", so each now claims only what the table below supports, or points to the skills frameworks instead.

What the adverts say about this track overall: Ghanaian demand for content creators, videographers and social content officers is real but concentrated in Accra and Tema, and many adverts state a diploma or degree. Short form vertical video editing is the most consistent demand in both local and remote adverts, and remote editing work usually expects a laptop and dependable internet. This is why the track stresses local client work as the first earning route and is honest about where a laptop becomes necessary.

## Framework skills

* **O*NET 27-4032.00 Film and Video Editors** (CC BY 4.0): arranging footage into a continuous story from scripts or instructions; adding music, dialogue and sound effects and correcting errors; selecting the best shots for a smooth logical story; reviewing footage before assembly; operating editing systems. Knowledge: communications and media, English language, computers and electronics, fine arts. Transferable skills: judgment and decision making, time management, coordination.
* **O*NET 27-4031.00 Camera Operators** (CC BY 4.0): composing and framing shots and operating cameras rated as the most important tasks; editing for broadcast as a supplemental task; knowledge of multimedia and communications.
* **Skills England Content creator standard** (OGL v3.0): duties include planning creative content against a brief, aligning content with brand strategy, writing for multiple media, creating visual and audio content, storing and organising content, managing content online, evaluating effectiveness and collaborating with clients. Knowledge topics include copyright and data protection, audience segmentation, tone of voice, platform audience behaviour, cross platform content, capturing visuals and audio, sourcing and editing media, content organisation and retrieval, search optimisation and campaign evaluation. Skills include interpreting briefs, storyboarding, pitching ideas, scriptwriting, accessible writing and interpreting data.
* **SydCrest catalogue**: lists content creation as a Tier A pick, phone native, earning through brand and SME work, with live streaming sales titles seen on Jobberman. One Ghana platform in the survey runs content creation as a cohort programme, so the track says learners can use it to prepare for such programmes without naming any provider.

## Gaps the adverts showed and what we did

There was no earlier outline for this track, so the outline was built from the research. Choices worth noting:

* **Live streaming and live selling** appeared in three Ghanaian adverts, so it is taught inside `short-video` rather than left out.
* **Thumbnails and graphics** appeared in four adverts; covered inside `export-and-organise` and `youtube-basics` rather than as a separate design node, because the graphic design track will go deeper.
* **Motion graphics and animation** (three adverts) are mostly in mid level or agency roles; mentioned only as on screen text and simple effects. A later optional node could add them.
* **Desktop tools (Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro)** appear in three adverts. The track teaches editing skills on free phone editors and says plainly that longer layered edits and many remote roles need a laptop, pointing to school labs, libraries and community centres.
* **Degrees and diplomas** appear in five adverts. The roadmap cannot provide those; the portfolio lesson and the honest earning routes lesson set expectations and stress client work as a first step.
* **Data saving** and **local languages** are not frequent advert keywords but are essential for SydCrest's learners and are supported by advert 17 and Skills England's accessible writing topic.
