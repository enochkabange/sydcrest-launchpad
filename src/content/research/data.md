# Research: Data Analyst track (`data-analyst`)

Issue #84. Researched and written 25 October 2026. Everything in the outline and lesson
content is original. roadmap.sh was not opened, searched or used, and no "<field> roadmap"
style searches were run. No logins; LinkedIn was not scraped. Job adverts were read for
which skills appear; no advert text is reproduced here or in the content.

## Who the track is for

Junior routes into data work in Ghana are wider than the job title "data analyst". The
adverts below show four doors: data entry and data clerk roles, M&E (monitoring and
evaluation) and MEAL officer roles at NGOs (many in Tamale and the northern regions),
analyst and BI roles at banks and telcos in Accra, and research assistant roles at
research organisations that run field surveys. Remote junior analyst roles open to
Africa based applicants exist but mostly ask for SQL plus a BI tool.

## Frameworks and curricula

* **O*NET Business Intelligence Analysts (F1):** tasks include producing reports and dashboards, maintaining them, documenting report specifications and spotting trends. Tools include Excel, Power BI, Looker, SPSS, SQL databases. Skills: critical thinking, writing, speaking, mathematics.
* **O*NET Data Entry Keyers (F2):** compare entries with source documents, find and correct errors, verify before entry. Tools: spreadsheets, Google Drive, Access style databases.
* **ESCO (F3):** the API and portal search were unavailable on the day; not used.
* **freeCodeCamp Data Analysis with Python (C1, BSD-3-Clause):** topic names seen include notebooks, reading CSV data, pandas DataFrames, conditional selection, creating columns, data cleaning and duplicates, visualisation, plus projects on descriptive statistics and demographic data. Used only to confirm which topics a Python for analysis node should cover; no text or ordering taken.
* **Official docs (D1 to D5):** confirmed that KoboToolbox and ODK both support XLSForm, offline phone collection and CSV or Excel export, and that Sheets supports pivot tables, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP and dropdown validation.

## Outline changes and why

Draft had 25 nodes; revised outline has 26.

* **Added an M&E section** (`monitoring-evaluation`, `indicators-frameworks`, `data-quality-reporting`). M&E frameworks and data quality assurance appear in five and seven adverts, mostly NGOs in Tamale and Accra, and the draft had no node for them. DHIS2 (A5) is mentioned inside `data-quality-reporting`, not given a node.
* **Split spreadsheets into a section** with `spreadsheet-formulas` and `lookups-pivots`. Spreadsheets are the most common signal, and pivot tables, lookups and data validation are named explicitly (A3, A8, A12).
* **Added `tidy-data`** (accurate data entry and tidy tables) under foundations. Data entry roles (A18, A19, F2) and data quality signals justify it, and it is a prerequisite for every later node.
* **Added `survey-design`** separate from `mobile-surveys`. Questionnaire design, sampling and baselines appear in A2, A4, A6, A10; the draft only covered the tool side.
* **Renamed `real-data` to `field-data`** ("Surveys and field data") to match the M&E and research assistant adverts.
* **Folded `asking-questions` into the `foundations` section explanation** and `presenting-findings` into the `communicating` section explanation, to keep the outline within budget while keeping both skills taught.
* **Folded `notebooks` into `python-basics`** (Colab is the setting for the Python lessons) and mentioned R as the free member of the statistics package family, since SPSS and Stata (seven adverts with the R group) are paid.
* **Dropped `intro-ml`.** One line in one 2024 advert (A1) as a plus; not a junior requirement.
* **Dropped `freelancing`** as its own node (no advert signal); short freelance projects for local businesses and NGOs are covered in `portfolio` and `job-search` as a way to build evidence.
* **Folded `ghana-data-sources` into `portfolio`**, where Ghanaian public data is used to build case studies.
* **Python moved after SQL and communicating.** Python appears in three adverts, SQL in seven and BI tools in eight, so the order now follows the evidence: spreadsheets, field data, M&E, SQL, dashboards, then Python.
