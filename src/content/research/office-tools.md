# Research: Office tools track (`office-tools`)

Track file: `src/content/roadmaps/office-tools.ts` (roadmap slug `office-tools`, track `office-tools`).
Lesson content: `src/content/roadmaps/content/office-tools.content.ts`.
Researched and written 7 November 2026 for issue #294.

## Why this track exists

Admin, office, secretarial and front desk adverts in Ghana keep asking for "Microsoft Office" (Word,
Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) and, more and more, for "Google Workspace" (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets,
Calendar, Meet). Our existing tracks assume these basics: `virtual-assistant` teaches inbox and
calendar management for clients, and `excel-power-bi` goes deep on spreadsheets. This beginner track
fills the gap between the Digital Skills Bridge and those tracks: everyday office software, used
well, on free accounts.

Learners pick **Google Workspace or Microsoft 365** at most steps. The roadmap supports this with
choice groups: two sibling nodes share a `choice_group` and the learner completes one. Shared ideas
that do not depend on the tool (professional email, safe sharing, reviewing documents) sit in their
own node so they are taught once.

## Method and provenance

* No roadmap.sh pages, no "<field> roadmap" searches, no logins, no LinkedIn.
* Job adverts were read for which software and tasks appear, through job board pages and search
  result snippets. No advert text is copied; the tables below record keywords only. Salary figures
  that appeared on some boards were deliberately not recorded. Some adverts carried gender
  preferences; those were not recorded and play no part in the track.
* Product facts (free storage, meeting limits, feature names, what needs a paid plan) were checked
  on official Google and Microsoft pages on 7 November 2026. Menus move, so lessons name features
  and say "at the time of writing" where a limit or menu path could change, and tell learners to
  check the current help page.
* GCFGlobal LearnFree series were chosen only from slugs listed in `https://learnfree.org/sitemap.xml`
  (read 7 November 2026), because that site returns 200 even for slugs that do not exist.
* Every resource URL in the track file returned 200 to a request on 7 November 2026.

## Outline decisions

25 nodes in six sections. Seven choice groups, each a Google option and a Microsoft option under the
same parent: `email-tool`, `file-tool`, `doc-tool`, `sheet-tool`, `slide-tool`, `form-tool` and
`meeting-tool`.

* **Section one teaches the choice itself:** which suite to pick, free accounts, what needs a paid
  plan, and that learning one makes the other easy. Employers name both (A5 A8 A9 for Google; most
  others for Microsoft).
* **Shared ideas are taught once:** professional email and phishing, safe sharing (anyone with the
  link), and reviewing (comments, suggestions, track changes) do not depend on the suite.
* **Spreadsheets stay basic** and point to `excel-power-bi` for depth, as the issue asks.
* **Calendar and video meetings are one choice node per suite,** because in practice the invite
  and the meeting link are made together (Google Calendar with Meet; Outlook calendar with Teams).
* **Free plans only:** every practice task works on a free Google account or a free Microsoft
  account with Microsoft 365 for the web. Lessons say plainly where a feature needs a paid plan
  (OneDrive link passwords and expiry, offline folders in the OneDrive phone app, longer group
  meetings, more form responses).
* **Data honesty:** video meetings use much more data than documents; lessons give camera off and
  audio only tips rather than figures.
* **Job titles in sections** come only from the adverts above. Rows marked "title and location only" count for job titles, never for skills.
