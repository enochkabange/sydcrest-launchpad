## Gaps found and changes made

Skills the adverts demanded that the draft outline lacked or underplayed:

* **Retries, timeouts and idempotency.** Named directly in fintech adverts
  (S13, S14) and central to mobile money on unreliable networks. Added
  `idempotency-and-retries` as its own node.
* **Asynchronous code.** Node.js adverts and payment adverts mention
  asynchronous processing. Added `async-programming`, which also sets up
  timeouts for later lessons.
* **Caching, Redis and NoSQL.** Appeared in payment company adverts and O*NET.
  Added optional `caching-and-nosql` under databases.
* **ORMs.** Named in adverts (Entity Framework, Prisma). Made
  `migrations-and-orms` required instead of optional.
* **Queues and background jobs.** Folded into `sms-and-whatsapp` (background
  sending with a worker) to stay within 30 nodes and keep sections at 2 to 4
  topics.

Nodes removed or merged:

* `choose-a-language` merged into the `a-language` section explanation; a
  separate node repeated it.
* `api-docs` folded into `rest-apis`. No advert asked for OpenAPI by name.
* `ussd` folded into `sms-and-whatsapp` as one paragraph. No advert or
  framework mentioned it, but it matters for reaching customers on basic
  phones, so it stays as context rather than a node.

Other changes: `auth` now follows `validation-and-errors`; the payments summary
no longer names specific providers; the section formerly called "Integrations
Ghanaian products need" is now "Payments, messaging and patchy networks". Node
count went from 28 to 28 (three removed, three added).
