# Research: Digital Sales track (`digital-sales`)

Tier B outline (issue #96). Researched and written 27 October 2026. Outline only: lessons are
marked "coming soon" by the site. Everything in `src/content/roadmaps/digital-sales.ts` is
original. roadmap.sh was not opened, searched or used, and no "<field> roadmap" style searches
were run. No logins; LinkedIn was not opened. Job adverts were read for which skills appear; no
advert text is reproduced. Salary and commission figures on some pages were ignored.

## Who the track is for

Sales is one of the largest Jobberman categories on the days read (P1, S1), and entry titles
include sales representative, sales executive, sales and marketing officer, graduate trainee
sales, field sales officer, telesales consultant, business development representative and lead
caller. Employers seen: internet providers, building materials, agrochemicals and seed firms,
manufacturers and distributors, fintechs, a betting app, SaaS and VoIP companies selling
remotely, HR and staffing firms. Blossom Academy runs an AI Powered Sales programme (C1). The
track is the digital side of selling: finding and qualifying leads, reaching them by phone,
WhatsApp and email, keeping a pipeline in a CRM or spreadsheet, and closing honestly.

It is kept distinct from the `digital-business` track, which is about running your own online
shop. This track is about selling for an employer or client.

Field category `business` rather than `marketing`: the adverts are sales and business
development roles, and the existing marketing tracks cover campaigns rather than one to one
selling.

## Frameworks and curricula

* **O*NET Sales Representatives of Services (F1, CC BY 4.0):** tasks include answering questions
  on prices and availability, preparing presentations and proposals, negotiating terms,
  completing agreements, and supporting clients after the sale. Tools: CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot),
  spreadsheets, office suites. Skills: active listening, speaking, persuasion, negotiation,
  service orientation, time management.
* **Blossom Academy AI Powered Sales (C1):** keywords on the page: prospecting, multichannel
  outreach (phone, email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp), lead qualification (BANT, an introduction to
  MEDDIC), discovery, consultative selling, objection handling, negotiation, closing,
  presentations, resilience and rejection, time management, responsible AI and customer data
  use; tools CRM, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Business, Sheets or Excel, AI assistants. Used to confirm
  topic areas only; no text or ordering taken.

## Outline decisions and why

28 nodes in seven sections.

* **Prospecting and outreach are separate sections** because finding leads and contacting them
  are the two most common signals.
* **WhatsApp selling is its own node**: it is how most Ghanaian customers prefer to talk, and C1
  names WhatsApp Business as a sales tool.
* **CRM and pipeline section** uses free CRMs and a spreadsheet so learners can practise on a
  phone; HubSpot, Zoho Bigin and Sheets are named only as examples.
* **Honest selling and customer data** is included because mis selling and pushy tactics harm
  customers and employers, and C1 covers responsible use of customer data. It also covers the
  Data Protection Act context (D1).
* **AI for sales is optional**: C1 leads with it, but adverts did not ask for it.
* No numbers about earnings or commission appear in learner copy.
