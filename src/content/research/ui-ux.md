## Framework skills

* **O*NET 15-1255.00 Web and Digital Interface Designers (F1):** user research to set design
  requirements and analyse feedback, visual design concepts, interface and site design, testing and
  fixing, collaboration with stakeholders, documentation; skills of active listening, critical
  thinking, writing, speaking, complex problem solving; tool categories include graphics and design
  software, web frameworks, analytics, collaboration and project management.
* **ESCO user interface designer (F2):** designs interfaces for applications and systems, covering
  layout, graphics and dialogue design and adaptation. The ESCO portal and API were unavailable on the
  day, so the essential and optional skills list could not be read. A follow up check is recommended.
* **GOV.UK Service Manual, user research (C1):** topics include learning user needs, non digital
  users, planning, finding participants, informed consent, research with disabled people, contextual
  observation, interviews, experience maps, moderated usability testing, pop up research, analysing
  and sharing findings.
* **W3C WAI fundamentals (C2):** accessibility principles, people with disabilities, essential
  components, user requirements.

## Gaps found and outline changes

Skills the adverts demanded that the draft outline lacked or underplayed, and what changed:

1. **Design systems were optional** but appear in 10 of 18 adverts. Made `design-systems` required.
2. **Personas and journey maps** (5 adverts) were missing. Renamed `synthesis` to "Personas, journeys
   and problem statements" and covered affinity mapping, personas, journey maps and how might we.
3. **Analytics, metrics and A/B tests** (5 adverts) were missing. Added `measuring-after-launch`.
4. **Heuristic review and UX audits** (2 adverts) were missing. Folded into `usability-testing`,
   renamed "Usability testing and expert review", rather than adding a node.
5. **Responsive design** (8 adverts) was implicit. Renamed `mobile-patterns` to "Mobile and
   responsive layouts" and covered breakpoints and mobile first.
6. **Surveys** (4 adverts) added to `user-interviews`, renamed "Interviews and quick surveys".
7. **Research planning, consent and recruitment** (A15, A16, C1) plus the brief's emphasis on research
   in markets: replaced `context-and-constraints` with `field-research` ("Research in markets and
   communities"), placed before interviews, covering planning, recruiting through trusted community
   members, consent in the person's language, Act 843 and contextual observation.
8. **USSD, SMS and low end phones** (S20, A11, brief): added `low-end-phones`. The old
   `low-bandwidth-design` became `offline-and-slow-data`, now centred on the moment the network drops.
9. **Tool neutrality:** renamed `figma` to `design-tools`, teaching Figma and Penpot as free examples.
10. **Presenting rationale** (7 adverts) built into `case-studies` and `job-search` instead of a new node.
11. Section `inclusive-design` retitled "Designing for real conditions in Ghana"; section `testing`
    retitled "Testing and shipping" to hold handoff and measurement.
12. `freelancing` kept optional and refocused on UI/UX work (forms, WhatsApp ordering, USSD flows,
    small reviews) rather than logos and flyers, which are graphic design.

Final count: 30 nodes (7 sections, 23 topics, 1 optional). Not added as nodes, mentioned in lessons
only: AI design to code tools (2 adverts), Agile and Jira (2 adverts), motion design (1 advert).

## Open questions for the founder

* No current, clearly junior UI/UX advert based in Tamale, Kumasi or another northern city was found;
  Ghana demand in the sample is Accra or remote. Lessons point learners to remote and NGO routes too.
* USSD design is supported by the brief (learner context) and two supplementary search snippets but by none of the 18 adverts directly. The reviewer kept the node because basic phones and USSD mobile money are part of the learners' own setting, and softened the lesson so it no longer says adverts mention USSD.
* The ESCO skills list could not be read on the day (service unavailable), including on a later retry by the reviewer.
* Ghana's Persons with Disability Act, 2006 (Act 715) is cited as current law. A replacement Persons with Disabilities Bill was still pending in July 2026; if it passes, update the accessibility lesson.
* CONTENT_GUIDE says `pass_threshold` is a count of correct answers, but the validator and database
  treat it as a percentage from 50 to 100. This track uses 75 for 4 questions and 80 for 5 (3 of 4,
  4 of 5).
