# Research: Product track (product-manager roadmap)

Issue #84. Researched and written 2026-10-25. Provenance: no roadmap.sh pages
were opened, searched for or reproduced, and no "product roadmap" style
searches were run. Adverts were read for which skills appear; no advert text
was copied. No logins, no LinkedIn scraping.

## Headline finding

True entry level product manager adverts are rare in Ghana. Across Jobberman
Ghana, company career pages and remote boards, almost every "Product Manager"
advert asked for several years of experience, and the remote roles open to
Ghana based applicants (Wave, M-KOPA) were senior. The junior doors that do
appear are adjacent roles: business analyst, information systems analyst,
product operations, product analyst or product executive, M&E / data analyst
in NGOs, and associate product roles at B2B tech firms. The skills those roles
ask for are dominated by requirements gathering, user stories and specs,
testing before go live, documentation and user training, data analysis with
spreadsheets (and sometimes SQL), and stakeholder communication.

The outline was revised to teach exactly those skills and to frame
`breaking-in` honestly around these adjacent routes.

## Framework skills

* **GOV.UK Service Manual (OGL v3).** Product manager: fit the service to organisational priorities, set direction, make sure user needs are met including accessibility, prioritise user stories each sprint, input on technical and design choices, sign off finished stories. Business analyst: define the business context and problem, analyse business areas, turn needs and constraints into user stories, identify improvements and success measures. Topic areas: agile delivery, user research, design, measuring success, technology, the team, accessibility.
* **O*NET 11-2021.00 (includes Product Manager title).** Tasks include new product development, reviewing product specifications, financial evaluation and pricing. Essential skills: active listening, speaking, critical thinking, monitoring. Technology: spreadsheets, CRM, BI and data analysis, presentation and project management software.
* **O*NET 13-1111.00 Management Analysts.** Gather and organise information on problems and procedures, analyse data, document findings and recommend changes. Essential skills include writing, active listening and critical thinking. Technology includes Excel and process diagram tools.
* **O*NET 15-2051.01 Business Intelligence Analysts.** Report generation, dashboards, trend analysis; Excel, Power BI, Looker.
* **ESCO ICT business analyst (description only).** Analyse and design an organisation's processes and systems, assess how technology fits the business, document requirements and see them through implementation.
