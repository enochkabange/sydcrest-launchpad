## Method and provenance

* Job adverts were read for which skills and tools appear. No advert text is copied anywhere in the roadmap or lessons; the tables below record keywords only.
* Several Jobberman listing URLs redirected to the category page, so for those adverts we saw the listing card (title, employer, location, short skill tags) rather than the full advert. The depth column says which.
* No roadmap.sh pages, no "field roadmap" searches, no logins, no LinkedIn pages. One fintech advert seen only on LinkedIn (a comms and growth associate role) was excluded.
* Salary bands shown on some adverts were deliberately not recorded.
* Pages that failed to load (404, 403 or a redirect to a category list) were used only through their search result snippet and are marked S in the depth column.

## Framework skills

* **O*NET Search Marketing Strategists (13-1161.01):** keyword research and search intent, SEO content, web metrics and KPIs, A/B testing, conversion improvement, paid search and social campaigns, competitor research, analytics setup; tools include Google Analytics, Google Ads, Tag Manager, WordPress, spreadsheets.
* **O*NET Public Relations Specialists (27-3031.00):** website and social media updates, press releases, newsletters, promotional material, community relations, campaign coordination; tools include Canva, Google Docs, Facebook, Google Analytics, WordPress.
* **ESCO digital marketing manager (via crosswalk, ISCO 1221):** digital strategy, brand awareness, social media, email marketing, marketing automation, SEO, online events, online advertising, KPI monitoring, competitor analysis, market research.
* **Google Skillshop topic titles:** Google Ads (Search, Display, Video, Measurement), Google Analytics, Google Business Profile (listed as Google My Business). The free Fundamentals of Digital Marketing course covers business strategy, web presence, search, SEO, search ads, local search, social media, display, video, email, content marketing, e commerce and analytics.
* **DMP cohort (`src/content/dmp/curriculum.ts`):** shared weeks cover channels, Google Business Profile, content calendars, social graphics, comment replies, analytics and A/B basics; specialisations go deeper on SEO (keyword research, on page, local listings), social media (ad targeting, video storytelling, influencer outreach) and Google Ads (search campaign setup, ad copy, bidding). This track teaches the beginner layer of all three so a learner can pick a specialisation with confidence, and stops short of the specialisation depth.

## Gaps found and what changed

* **Email marketing** appeared in seven adverts and in ESCO but had no node. Added `email-marketing` under the renamed section "Social media, messaging and email".
* **Live streaming and live selling** appeared in retail and health products adverts. Merged `phone-photo-video` and the optional `short-video-platforms` into two nodes: `graphics-and-photos` and `short-video` (now covering TikTok, Reels, Status and live selling, and required).
* **Campaign planning** appeared in four adverts and DMP week 10. Added `campaign-plan` under the measurement section, retitled "Campaigns and results".
* **Certificates and job applications**: certifications appear as a hiring signal and Jobberman warns against paying for jobs. Added `job-applications`.
* **Market research and competitor monitoring** folded into `know-your-audience` (retitled).
* **Influencer and creator partnerships** folded into `community-management` rather than a separate node, since only senior adverts ask for it and the DMP social specialisation covers it in depth.
* **Brand consistency and AI drafting tools** folded into `copywriting` and `graphics-and-photos`.
* **Search ads** changed from optional to required, since it appears alongside social ads in adverts and is a DMP specialisation.
* **Dropped `simple-websites`** (optional): website and CMS work appeared only in two adverts, one senior; the frontend track covers building sites, and the Google Business Profile and SEO lessons cover being findable without a site. This kept the track at 30 nodes.
