# Research: Digital Business and Ecommerce track

Track file: `src/content/roadmaps/digital-business.ts` (roadmap slug `digital-business`).
Lesson content: `src/content/roadmaps/content/digital-business.content.ts`.
Researched 25 October 2026 for the wave 3 issue (new track, no earlier outline).

## Method and provenance

* This is a new track, so the outline was written from scratch against the sources below. Positioning follows `Planning/12_TRACK_CATALOGUE.md` (rank 9, "Digital business and e-commerce"): Sales and Management and Business Development were the two largest Jobberman Ghana categories on the day read, many northern learners already trade, and only Google's combined Digital Marketing and E-commerce certificate covers this path among the platforms surveyed.
* Job adverts were read for which skills and tools appear. No advert text is copied anywhere in the roadmap or lessons; the tables below record keywords only.
* Several Jobberman listing URLs redirected to a category page, so for those adverts we used the listing card or the search result snippet. The depth column says which.
* No roadmap.sh pages, no "field roadmap" searches, no logins, no LinkedIn pages.
* Salary and pay figures shown on some adverts and snippets were deliberately not recorded.
* Two e-commerce listings seen on the Nigerian Jobberman site (a Lagos based store and an internship with Nigerian marketplaces) were excluded because the brief asks for Ghanaian or Africa friendly remote roles.
* Official Ghanaian sources (ORC, GRA, Bank of Ghana, FDA) were read only to fact check the formal business lessons. Lessons stay general and send learners to the official page for current fees, thresholds and steps, because these change and secondary sources disagreed (for example on the business name registration fee).

## Framework skills

* **O*NET Online Merchants (13-1199.06), the closest match to this track:** order fulfilment and shipping, payment processing, order and invoice management, customer support, sourcing and purchasing merchandise, pricing and cost calculation, product listings and descriptions, product photography, inventory ordering, online marketing, sales and expense tracking, customer databases, online security and privacy, business planning. Tools include accounting software, payment services, spreadsheets, website builders.
* **O*NET Retail Salespersons (41-2031.00):** greeting customers and finding needs, recommending products, pricing and taking payment, product demonstration, displays and promotion, inventory, returns and exchanges, security and theft prevention, sales records, delivery arrangements. Skills: persuasion, service orientation, negotiation, mathematics.
* **O*NET Customer Service Representatives (43-4051.00):** answering enquiries, recording interactions, checking problems are resolved, contacting customers with updates, resolving complaints, referring unresolved issues, comparing returns with invoices.
* **Google Digital Marketing and E-commerce certificate (topic titles only):** foundations of digital marketing and e-commerce, attracting and engaging customers, interacting with customers online, email marketing, analytics and measurement, building and managing e-commerce stores, customer loyalty. Listed skills include order fulfilment, loyalty programmes, e-commerce, social media marketing, web presence.
* **Official Ghanaian sources:** ORC registers business names (sole proprietorships) and companies; GRA treats the Ghana Card PIN as the TIN for individuals and runs a Modified Taxation Scheme for informal sector traders; the Bank of Ghana warns that no legitimate provider asks for a PIN, password or OTP; the FDA regulates food and cosmetics, and news reports from Tamale name labelling as the main hurdle for shea cosmetic SMEs.

## Gaps found and decisions

* **Live selling** appeared in four adverts (11, 13, 14, 15). It is folded into `social-and-live-selling` rather than a separate node, because it rests on the same skills as social selling and the track is near the 30 node limit.
* **Telesales, CRM tools, pipelines and targets** appear mainly in employment adverts rather than in running your own shop. They are taught in `sales-jobs` (and touched in `repeat-customers` as a simple customer list), so the track serves both traders and job seekers.
* **Stock and inventory** appeared in four adverts and both retail frameworks, so it has its own node (`stock-control`) rather than sitting inside bookkeeping.
* **Paid ads** appeared in one advert only. It is mentioned in `low-cost-marketing` as something to try after free channels work, and the digital marketer track is the place to go deeper.
* **Field sales and local languages** (adverts 2, 9) are mentioned in `sales-jobs` and `customers-and-competitors` rather than given nodes.
* **Product standards** is optional: it matters for food, cosmetics and similar goods (very relevant to shea butter and food sellers in the north) but not for every seller.
* **Field category** is set to `marketing` because the admin editor only accepts the existing categories; a `business` category would need an app change, which is outside this content PR.
