# PageHeader

The top of a marketing page: accent, eyebrow, h1 and lead; with `aside` it becomes a split hero from lg.

## When to use

Use once per public page. Title uses text-h1, the lead text-lead in max-w-reading. Already wraps itself in Container.

## Props

eyebrow: string; title: string; lead: ReactNode; aside?: ReactNode.

## Where it is used

/events, /experts, /skills, /business, /about and other public pages

## Source

`src/components/marketing/section.tsx`. Exported as `PageHeader`.
