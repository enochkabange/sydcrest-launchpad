# GoalRing

This week's goal as a ring: topics passed out of the goal, drawn with the progress tokens.

## When to use

Use inside the This week card. The ring turns progress-complete once the goal is met; the numbers and the progressbar role carry the meaning.

## Props

done: number; target: number.

## Where it is used

ThisWeek (dashboard)
