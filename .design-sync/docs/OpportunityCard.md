# OpportunityCard

One opportunity on the board: kind, your status, title, organisation, summary, where and until when. The whole card is the link.

## When to use

Use on /opportunities and in suggestions. Kind and status are words, never colour alone.

## Props

o: Opportunity; today: string (YYYY-MM-DD); status?: "saved" | "applied"; headingLevel?: "h2" | "h3".

## Where it is used

/opportunities, SuggestedForYou

## Source

`src/components/opportunities/opportunity-card.tsx`. Exported as `OpportunityCard`.
