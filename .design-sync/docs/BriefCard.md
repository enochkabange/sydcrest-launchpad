# BriefCard

A brief on the board: kind, title, description, skills, who posted it and the facts. The whole card is one link.

## When to use

Use in the /briefs grid. Who posted shows initials or the organisation icon, never an invented photo. Facts show only when they have a value.

## Props

brief: Brief (a brief_board row; see index.d.ts).

## Where it is used

/briefs
