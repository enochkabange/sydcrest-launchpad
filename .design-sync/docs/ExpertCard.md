# ExpertCard

An expert in the directory: initials or their own photo, headline, trust badges, skills, honest trust facts and what they offer. The whole card is one link.

## When to use

Use in the /experts grid (AutoGrid). Trust facts appear only when real rows back them: a new expert says "New on SydCrest, no reviews yet".

## Props

expert: DirectoryExpert; featured?: boolean.

## Where it is used

/experts

## Source

`src/components/experts/expert-card.tsx`. Exported as `ExpertCard`.

## Notes

- Previews never show a star rating: ratings appear only after real reviews.
