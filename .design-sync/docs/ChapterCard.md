# ChapterCard

A chapter in the list: status, place, name, summary and who leads it. The whole card is the link.

## When to use

Use in the /chapters grid. With no lead it says "Looking for a chapter lead".

## Props

chapter: PublicChapter; leads: ChapterPerson[].

## Where it is used

/chapters

## Source

`src/components/chapters/chapter-bits.tsx`. Exported as `ChapterCard`.
