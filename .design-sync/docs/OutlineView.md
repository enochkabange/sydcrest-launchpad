# OutlineView

The List renderer: section cards with their topics as numbered rows, two columns from lg.

## When to use

The second view in ROADMAP_VIEWS, chosen with the Map and List switcher and remembered on the device.

## Props

slug: string; view: RoadmapView.

## Where it is used

/roadmaps/[slug]
